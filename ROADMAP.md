# Roadmap

The release candidate focuses on durable investigation: ingestion, immutable evidence,
bounded analysis, case/campaign work, explainable IOC correlation and reviewed decisions.
The private Cloud adapter adds organization isolation, verified signup, trials,
subscriptions, retention and signed outgoing events.

Future work: inbound email/reporting mailbox, Outlook/Gmail add-ins, richer SSO
connectors, SAML/LDAP, passkeys, MSSP delegation, dynamic malware detonation,
STIX/SIEM connectors and distributed storage/HA. These are not shipped capabilities.
Do not market an unavailable feature as included in a current plan.

La V1 n’intègre pas de collecte email entrante ni de compléments de messagerie.
Community reste utilisable seule ; les fonctions commerciales sont privées.
